# Photo split record

photo_splits.csv lists the 14,892 photo IDs, community labels, split/group IDs,
input-byte hashes and source download status used in this experiment.
It is a reduced copy of the original split table, not a drop-in replacement
for the SHA-bound training manifests. No photographs, coordinates or
observer account details are included. Observation IDs link back to
https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/ID and photo IDs to
https://www.inaturalist.org/photos/ID. Identifications and licences on
those pages may have changed since acquisition.

The original licence/attribution records and full manifests remain archived
locally. Do not assume the code licence applies to the source photographs
or other third-party material. The hortorum source remains INCOMPLETE.
